REFORM-3D — Volumetric identity

The mark shows three open isometric planes framing a fixed central volume.

Jade: acquisition robustness.
Muted coral: cross-modal alignment.
Warm gold: anatomical generalization.
Central cube: the frozen 3D encoder being evaluated.

Use reform3d-volumetric-logo.svg on light backgrounds.
Use reform3d-volumetric-logo-dark.svg on dark backgrounds.
The matching wordmark files include the framework name as outlined paths.
The monochrome and white marks work for single-color reproduction.

All SVG backgrounds and negative spaces are transparent.
All geometry and lettering are vectors. There are no linked fonts or images.
